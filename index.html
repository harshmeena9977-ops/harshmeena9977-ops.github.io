Method

The analyst loop

The same five steps, every time — not corporate experience, but a repeatable process.

01

Frame the question

Before opening SQL, define the decision this analysis needs to support.

02

Clean the evidence

Messy, real data — missing values, inconsistent types, duplicate rows — gets resolved before anything is trusted.

03

Explore the signal

Query, segment, and visualize until a pattern holds up across multiple cuts of the data.

04

Test the finding

A pattern is a hypothesis, not a conclusion, until it's checked against alternative explanations.

05

Communicate the decision

The output is a dashboard or a recommendation a non-technical stakeholder can act on immediately.
